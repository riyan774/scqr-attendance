    How Present and Late are decided

    Every class period has its own start time and its own grace time. You set both when you create a section on the Dashboard.

    Example: afternoon shift. 1st period starts 12:45 PM with 15 min grace. 2nd period starts 2:20 PM with 5 min grace.
    PresentBefore the grace time ends1st period: scanned 12:45 PM up to 12:59 PM. 2nd period: scanned up to 2:24 PM.
    LateAfter the grace time1st period: 1:00 PM or later. 2nd period: 2:25 PM or later.
    AbsentNever scannedNot scanned before that period ends.
